VICE HARBOR

Golden hour. One harbour. Every car is an opportunity.

On foot — WASD move · mouse drag or Q / E orbit camera · Shift sprint · click to fire the pistol
Cars — walk up and press E to take one · WASD drive · Space handbrake · E again to get out
The law — theft, gunfire and collisions raise your stars, 0 to 5. Police pursue, and at 3 stars they shoot back. Stay out of sight to cool down, or get caught and pay the fine.
Work — follow the gold marker: a timed delivery, an escape to the Hideout, then a five-gate harbour run. Cash and progress save automatically.
Pause — Esc or P.
